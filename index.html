Methodology

A wiki on publicly traded companies to manage exposure.

A pure play is a publicly traded company that carries the same risk factors as a private investment you already hold. Short the pure play and if the shared risk materializes — the event that sinks your private holding also sinks the public one — your hedge pays off. These 30 portfolios group 2,301 companies from 2019 SEC filings by the risks they share, so you can find the pure-play hedge for whatever private exposure you carry.

Why 2019 data?

These risk profiles are built from pre-COVID 10-K filings. After 2020, nearly every company added "pandemic" as a top risk factor, flattening the signal. The 2019 baseline is a cleaner picture of the structural risks that actually distinguish one business from another.
